# Credits

This skill is [Matt Pocock](https://github.com/mattpocock)'s [`grilling`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills), copied word for word and renamed. One paragraph is mine: the last, which writes the settled decisions to `~/.claude/grilling/<branch>.md` for `review-change` to read. The design tree, the rounds, the frontier, the split between a **default** you state and an **open question** you ask, the reply format, and the rule that facts are the agent's job and decisions are the user's — all his.

It is vendored rather than pointed at so this repo stands on its own: `spec-griller` calls it, and without it that skill loses the interview that is its reason for existing. A copy under my own name is mine to maintain; the attribution a dependency would have carried lives here instead.
