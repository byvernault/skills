# Credits

The smell baseline in [`SMELLS.md`](SMELLS.md) comes from [Matt Pocock](https://github.com/mattpocock)'s [`code-review`](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) skill, reproduced word for word: the twelve entries, their *what it is* → *how to fix* shape, and the two rules that bind them (the repo overrides, and every smell stays a judgement call). The smells themselves are Martin Fowler's, from _Refactoring_, chapter 3; Matt's contribution is the one-line fix attached to each and the framing that makes them usable against a diff.

The closing *Why the axes stay apart* follows the same skill's *Why two axes*, extended from two axes to three.

The rest of this skill — the three axes, the brief, the false-positive list, the validation pass and the re-review path — came from `review-to-pr`, where it was written for this workflow, and moved here so there is one reviewer rather than two.

`code-review` does not depend on Matt's skill: the baseline is copied in rather than pointed at, and this file is the attribution a dependency would otherwise have carried.
